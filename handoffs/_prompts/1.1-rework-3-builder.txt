WHY THIS EXISTS:

  obligation_rules.yaml is authored, merged and correct — six rules reading
  policy + permissive_value. PolicyOption has NO value field at all: key,
  category, cost, effects, provenance, and nothing else. So permissive_value
  is a string pointing at nothing, and the audit confirmed no keying of
  policies fixes it, because the missing thing is the vocabulary itself.

  The whole Chapter 4 ethics layer is authored, merged, and inert.

  Without these fields the engine cannot tell whether a team has moved off the
  permissive default — which is the entire mechanism by which an ignored
  obligation arms an event.

BUILD — exactly two fields:

  options: list[SnakeKey] = Field(default_factory=list)
      the states this switch can be in, e.g. [minimal, standard, indefinite]
      Ordering is authoring convention, NOT semantics. Do not infer strictness
      from position.

  default: SnakeKey | None = None
      the state a team holds by NOT deciding. It is what makes the ethics
      layer cost something to ignore rather than something to opt into.
      Usually the same value an obligation names as permissive, but they are
      DISTINCT CONCEPTS and stay separate fields: default is where you start,
      permissive_value is what obliges. A pack may legitimately start a team
      somewhere already compliant.

  ONE constraint, at the model:
      options non-empty  ->  default MUST be a member of options

  Enforce nothing else. Do NOT require options to be non-empty. Do NOT require
  default when options is empty.
