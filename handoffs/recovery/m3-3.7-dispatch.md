# M3.7 Review & Lock dispatch

Implement the Review surface over the versioned sheet/preview boundary. Group commands by
their typed category, derive costs and warnings from `SimulationService` preview output,
and expose the current revision and lock state. The lock action must call
`SimulationService.lock` and preserve existing schedule participant state; do not add a
browser-side calculator or a second lock implementation.

The frontend owns `/review`, the decision sheet, warnings mirror, locked banner, and lock
action. Warnings inform the student and never block locking. Review does not claim round
advancement or debrief output.
