# M3.8 Debrief dispatch

Implement Debrief as a read-only report over immutable `RoundResult` rows. Preserve the
engine's score, scorecard, capability decomposition, events, state changes, accounting,
technical debt, and TCO evidence; do not recompute or narrate new numbers in the UI.

The frontend owns `/debrief`, the latest-round explanation, persisted-round history as
available, and a downloadable text report. Empty results stay explicit. M3.8 does not add
new scoring, transition, or advancement logic.
