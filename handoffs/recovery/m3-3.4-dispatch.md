# M3.4 Platform dispatch

## Scope

Build the Platform hosting and firm-wide service surface against the versioned runtime.

Allowed files:

- `backend/app/api/runtime_platform.py`
- `backend/app/main.py`
- `backend/tests/test_platform_runtime_api.py`
- `frontend/src/pages/Platform.jsx`
- `frontend/src/pages/Shell.jsx`
- `frontend/src/App.jsx`
- `frontend/src/components/AppShell.jsx`
- `frontend/src/styles/theme.css`
- `docs/frontend-platform.md`
- this dispatch and `m3-3.4-dod.md`

The patch is limited to `platform_service` commands. It must use the registered pack and
`SimulationService`; it must not duplicate transition or affordability logic. Student routes
derive the team from active enrollment and ignore a caller's cross-team selection.

## Acceptance evidence

1. API test proves scoped persisted service state and honest empty behavior.
2. Frontend lint and production build pass.
3. Browser probe at 1024px sees the Platform heading, hosting service, missing-service panel,
   active Platform nav link, and no horizontal overflow or page error.
