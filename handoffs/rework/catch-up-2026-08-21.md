## 1. Scope — what a builder must fix

### 1.1 `B13` — contradictory headcount on a live scoring input · **PRIORITY**

`backend/packs/riverside_grocery/catalog.yaml` authors `pos_system_2011.people_affected.count: 140`.
`mockups/components.html` shows 62. One of them is wrong and nobody has ruled which.

This is **not** cosmetic. `people_affected` is the denominator of the Organisational-Readiness
training sub-factor:

```
backend/app/engine/organisation.py:63
    training = clamp(dep.trained_count / dep.people_affected) if dep.people_affected else 0.0
```

Org is one of the three multiplicative factors in `Realised Value`. If 62 is the true headcount,
every training score for that component is wrong by a factor of 2.26, and the 1.4 pin
(`0.507` Org) was computed on the wrong denominator.

**Required:** establish the harvested truth from `mis_lite`, author one value, cite its source in
`PROVENANCE.md`, and make `components.html` agree.
**Closing check:** `grep people_affected` across pack and mockups yields one consistent figure;
the 1.4 Org pin is re-derived and either unchanged or re-pinned deliberately.
