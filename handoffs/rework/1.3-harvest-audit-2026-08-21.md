## Finding `1.3-RA-001` — authored FTE estimates are not marked at the values

Severity: Data

`preferences/services.yaml` bases stakeholder views on support-tier FTE equivalents, while
`platform.yaml` supplies those values as authored estimates. The strongest service
preference therefore rests on estimates whose calibration status is not attached to each
numeric producer. `GOVERNANCE.md §4.9` requires estimates to carry rationale and values that
cannot be justified to be marked and reported.

### Required rework

Attach a source/rationale to each `support_tiers[].fte_equivalent` value, or mark the values
`TODO: calibrate` and add them to the exact inventory in `PROVENANCE.md §7`. Do not treat a
general file-level statement as provenance for three independently consumed values.

## Finding `1.3-RA-002` — harvested raw fit remains adjacent to authoritative weights

Severity: Report / downstream-risk

`strategies.yaml` stores `harvested_raw_fit` beside `capability_weights`, although
`CONTRACTS.md` says the prior multipliers are a different scheme and must not be mixed.
`PROVENANCE.md` explains the distinction, but no machine check prevents a scorer from
consuming the wrong map.

### Required rework

Add a validator or schema-level guard that makes the provenance-only status executable,
and add a 1.4 test proving scoring reads `capability_weights` and never
`harvested_raw_fit`. Do not rename or remove the field without a versioned schema decision.

### Acceptance

Riverside remains validator-clean; all authored FTE values are individually justified or
listed for calibration; and a failing test demonstrates that substituting raw fit values
cannot silently change scoring inputs.
