## Functional

### 0.3-013 — The badge scale contradicts itself across screens, and on Situation two different states share one colour

The same label is bound to different status roles in different files:

```
                situation.html        platform.html            applications.html
Complete        pill ok               pill                     pill  ×5
Partly done     pill ok        ←!     pill ×2 · pill warn ×1    pill  ×3
Needs attention —                     pill danger              pill danger ×2 · pill warn ×2
```

Three consequences, in ascending order of cost:

1. `Complete` is green on Situation and neutral everywhere else; `Needs attention` is red in
   three places and amber in two.
2. **On Situation, `Partly done` and `Complete` are both `pill ok` — the same green.**
   ORDER FULFILMENT, at 25% realised value and explicitly "Held back by: Organisation",
   carries the same visual signal as POINT OF SALE at 5/5 coverage. A reader scanning colour
   sees no difference between a throttled capability and a healthy one. Verified in the
   1440 render.
3. These mockups are the acceptance criterion for the `StatusBadge` component at **0.5**.
   A contradictory scale here becomes a contradictory component contract there.

Spec §5.1 is explicit that status has its own scale and that accent must not be reused for
it; the roles exist and resolve correctly. This is the mockups binding the wrong ones.

### 0.3-014 — The SaaS option shows one of its two required warnings; the other was moved to a different mode

§5.6 names three warnings and assigns two of them to the same option:

```
onprem warning   "This would use all remaining platform capacity."
saas warning 1   "Inventory data would leave your systems."
saas warning 2   "A fourth separate login — you have no central sign-on."
```

As built (`mockups/applications.html`, wizard block):

```
On our on-premises platform   → onprem warning        ✓
On our cloud subscription     → saas warning 1        ← reassigned
Bought as a service           → saas warning 2 only   ← one warning, not two
```

Checklist **B11** reads: *"What does the SaaS option really cost? — both warnings visible
before 'Add to plan'."* As built it fails.

**Root cause is a spec gap, and the response to it is the finding.** §5.3 requires three
deployment modes; §5.6 supplies copy for only two of them. The builder resolved the gap by
reassigning a string rather than stopping — `GOVERNANCE.md §4.4`, *"a builder that
encounters an unspecified case stops and reports; obvious is not a category"* — and the
reassignment is not listed in the DoD's I5 residue table, so it is not disclosed either.
The fix needs an author decision on what the cloud-subscription mode's warning says, not a
builder guess.

## UX

### 0.3-015 — "Weighted by your strategy" appears as a panel subtitle, asserting that every primary activity is weighted

`applications.html:76` places it as a bare `<p>` under the **Primary activities** heading,
then again on Operations and Outbound logistics as their markers — three occurrences on one
panel. §5.4 weights exactly two activities:

```
Primary   Inbound logistics 1/4 · Operations 2/4 ▸ · Outbound logistics 4/5 ▸
          Marketing & sales 5/5 · Service 0/4        ▸ = weighted by declared strategy
```

The Support activities panel has no equivalent line, so the asymmetry reads as meaning
rather than decoration. Checklist **B9** — *"Where should I invest, given my strategy?"* —
turns on precisely this distinction.

### 0.3-016 — The two strategy-weighted activities are the only ones with no coverage badge

Every other activity carries one (`Partly done`, `Complete`, `Needs attention`,
`Not started`); on Operations and Outbound logistics the strategy marker occupies that slot
instead. The two activities the strategy says matter most are the two whose status cannot be
read at a glance. Checklist **B8** — *"Which parts of the business are we good at?"* — asks
for exactly that scan.

### 0.3-017 — `strategy weight 0.10` is a bare engine number on a student screen

Rendered on the CUSTOMER INSIGHT card. It is in §5.4, so it is not an invented figure — but
`GOVERNANCE.md §2.1` asks what it costs, who it affects, and what happens if it fails, and a
normalised weight answers none of the three. **O3** settled percentages for realised value
and decimals for the three factors that are multiplied together; a strategy weight is
neither of those cases and was never covered. Compare the same card's "not built ·
coverage 0/4", which reads correctly.

## Data

### 0.3-018 — The two Situation states disagree about the size of the programme budget, on one page

```
State: ready                      CAPITAL REMAINING   $44,000 of $220,000
State: round 1, before any results CAPITAL REMAINING  $400,000 of $400,000
```

Same company, same six-round run, stacked one above the other in the 1440 render. The
denominator is the total programme budget; it cannot be $400,000 in round 1 and $220,000 by
round 3.

**Not the builder's defect** — both figures are quoted exactly from the spec (§5.4 fixed
data at the round-3 block, and the `STATE — Situation, round 1` block added in v2). D1 says
figures must match §5.4 and they do. **Owner: author.** It matters beyond the mockup: the
budget model this feeds needs one total, and a reader running checklist **B4** ("Is the
budget healthy?") across the two states cannot reconcile them.

## Report

### 0.3-019 — I9's command cannot return clean, and the builder's deviation was correct

Spec I9: `grep -rniE "googleapis|gstatic|fonts\.google" frontend/ mockups/` → expect zero.
Run as written against the real tree it returns **32 hits, every one inside
`frontend/node_modules/`** — untracked dependency source:

```
31 × gstatic     e.g. existingStaticNonFields
                 frontend/node_modules/@babel/helpers/lib/helpers/applyDecs.js:364
                 ("existin·gStatic·NonFields" matches -i gstatic)
 1 × googleapis  frontend/node_modules/ajv/README.md
```

The builder recorded this as a DEVIATION with that exact identifier cited, and **it is
accurate** — I initially read it as a bad citation because my own `grep -r` was silently not
traversing `node_modules`, and only found the match after verifying the traversal itself.
The deviation was reported rather than worked around silently, which is the behaviour the
protocol asks for.

**The spec is what needs fixing.** Scoped to tracked files the invariant returns clean, and
that is the check it always meant:

```
$ git ls-files frontend mockups | xargs -r grep -lniE "googleapis|gstatic|fonts\.google"
  (no output)
```

Also verified independently: `frontend/index.html` no longer contains the Google Fonts
`<link>`/`preconnect` block at all, and no screen attempted a single non-`file://` request.

# Addendum — the spec was replaced while this audit was running

Discovered on committing: `main` carries **spec v3** (`22ee67d`), merged into this branch as
`33c89cc` *after* the audited commit `d492403`. **v3 respecs the pilot to three different
screens** — Platform, Components, Rollout — with a six-step Components wizard.

Everything above judged `d492403` against **v2.2, the spec the builder actually built to**,
and stands as a record of that. But its shelf life is now short, and pretending otherwise
would be the more misleading choice:

| Finding | Status under v3 |
|---|---|
| **0.3-013** badge scale | **Survives intact.** It is a defect in the status vocabulary itself, which any screen set inherits and which becomes 0.5's `StatusBadge` contract regardless of which three screens exist |
| 0.3-014 SaaS warnings · 0.3-015 strategy subtitle · 0.3-016 missing badges · 0.3-017 `strategy weight 0.10` · 0.3-018 budget denominator | **Moot as written** — they describe Situation and Applications, which v3 deletes. **Carry the causes, not the text:** v3's six-step wizard again enumerates options (Step 1, Step 2) that §5.7 must supply copy for, and 0.3-014's root cause was copy for two of three options. Re-test on the new screens |
| 0.3-019 I9 command | **Superseded and worse** — see below |

**v3 also reverses a settled decision and fires on the delivered work.** New **I8** requires
*one state per file* (`grep -cE "State:" mockups/*.html` → zero), reversing v2's O2 stacked
labelled states — the delivered mockups stack them. New **I9**, *no scores on decision
pages*, fires on the delivered `situation.html:87` and `:131` (`Tech 0.75 · Org 0.51 · Mgmt
0.65`, `Held back by: Organisation`). Both are consequences of the respec, not defects in a
build that predates it.

### 0.3-020 — the v3 renumber dropped the standing guards on fonts, and nothing replaced them

v2.2 carried two invariants that v3 reuses those numbers for:

```
v2.2  I9   no third-party font request anywhere   grep googleapis|gstatic over frontend/ mockups/
v2.2  I10  both faces + licence shipped           git ls-files "frontend/src/styles/fonts/*"
v3    I9   no scores on decision pages            (unrelated)
v3    I10  no bulk control on Rollout             (unrelated)
```

What remains is **pre-flight row 3** — `grep -c "googleapis|gstatic" frontend/index.html` — a
one-time check at build start, not a standing invariant. Consequences, both silent:

1. Nothing prevents a Google Fonts `<link>` reappearing in `frontend/index.html`. Removing it
   was the substance of the last rework cycle.
2. Nothing checks that the five `woff2` files **and `LICENSE.txt` stay shipped.** The SIL
   Open Font Licence requires the licence to accompany the fonts; a future commit could drop
   it and no check would notice.

**Owner: author.** Restore both as standing invariants under free numbers — the checks
themselves are already written and verified in this audit, and I9's should be scoped to
tracked files per 0.3-019.

### The process point, which is not mine to resolve

A spec was rewritten while its build sat in audit. The build is sound against the spec it
was given; roughly half of what it built is no longer specified. **Someone has to decide
whether `situation.html` and `applications.html` are discarded or retained as superseded
artifacts** — and if discarded, whether 0.3-013's fix is applied to the new screens instead
of the old. That is a scoping call, not an audit finding, and it should be made before a
builder is handed anything.

**What went well, recorded because the protocol otherwise only records failures.** The
offline self-containment is complete — not one non-`file://` request from any screen, with
the house typeface genuinely loading from vendored files. The I9 deviation was surfaced with
its evidence instead of being quietly scoped away. The phase discipline held: nine
screenshots, three states per screen, and mockups 4–10 untouched.
