## Finding 1.3-HR-001 — calibration prose asserts an invariant the evidence cannot prove

**Severity:** rework before merge  
**Type:** provenance accuracy / unsupported assumption  
**Files:**

- `backend/packs/riverside_grocery/PROVENANCE.md`, section 10.3
- `backend/packs/riverside_grocery/preferences/services.yaml`, introductory comments

The rework correctly says the FTE figures are authored estimates and need calibration, but
then claims premium will exceed the 2.0 starting pool under **“any plausible calibration”**
because premium is the top tier.

That conclusion does not follow from the available evidence. “Top tier” establishes an
ordinal relationship among support tiers; it does not establish a lower bound greater than
2.0 FTE. A future evidence-based calibration could preserve
`basic < standard < premium` while placing premium at or below 2.0. The harvested source
contains no FTE field with which to prove otherwise.

This also conflicts with the stated purpose of marking 2.4 for calibration: the exact
threshold relationship is currently authored, not invariant. Under the project’s
no-assumption protocol, the prose must distinguish the design-stated preference for premium
from the provisional numerical rationale.

### Required correction

Make the two passages prospective/conditional. Preserve these verified facts:

1. design/07 states that IT prefers `premium`;
2. premium is the highest declared support tier;
3. the current authored estimate, 2.4 FTE, exceeds the current 2.0 starting pool;
4. calibration may change both the margin **and whether that numerical threshold is crossed**;
5. calibration does not by itself change the design-stated `ideal_tier: premium` unless the
   underlying design contract is separately revised.

Do not change an FTE value, preference, weight, scorer, validator, or test to close this
finding. This is a two-location documentation/provenance correction.
