## Functional

### 0.4-001 — Three different selection grammars for one concept, across a set that must define exactly one

| Treatment | Where |
|---|---|
| Radio glyphs `●` / `○` | `services.html` only (6 occurrences; zero in the other eighteen files) |
| Highlighted card + highlighted chip | `challenges-item.html` — `Fund` tinted and blue-bordered, `Strategic priority` chip blue |
| **Nothing at all** | `rollout-detail.html`, `components-wizard.html` — flat bordered boxes, no chosen state |

The third case is finding **0.3-025**, filed 2026-07-27 and still open. So the set now shows
a reader three answers to "which one is selected?", and B17 fails on it.

This matters more than a normal consistency nit because of what these files are *for*: 0.5
builds the component library **from** them. Three grammars means either three components or
an arbitrary choice made by a component author — which is exactly the drift
`GOVERNANCE.md §4.3` forbids. One treatment should be chosen here, then applied backwards to
0.3's two screens.

### 0.4-002 — Review states two different capital positions, and its own commitment exceeds the money it says is available

On one screen, in one pass:

```
budget strip     CAPITAL THIS ROUND   $44,000 remaining of $220,000
totals line      Capital committed $174,000 of $220,000 · Remaining $46,000
```

The area table sums correctly — 0 + 98,000 + 34,000 + 30,000 + 0 + 0 + 12,000 = **$174,000**
— and 220,000 − 174,000 = **$46,000**, so the totals line is internally consistent. It cannot
be reconciled with the strip:

- **$44,000 ≠ $46,000.** Two capital-remaining figures, one screen.
- **$174,000 committed against $44,000 available.** If the strip is right, this round commits
  roughly four times the money left and nothing on the screen says so.

The run-rate arithmetic *does* reconcile — $58,300 + $3,900 = $62,200, matching "was
$58,300" — which makes the capital line's failure stand out rather than read as noise.

**Owner: author. Both figures are spec-sourced** — `spec.md:253` fixes the strip at "$44,000
remaining of $220,000" as shared chrome, `spec.md:317` fixes Review's totals at "$174,000 of
$220,000 · Remaining $46,000". The builder rendered both faithfully; the contradiction is in
§5.4.

This is the commit gate. B14 asks whether a reader knows what they are committing to, and a
careful one gets two incompatible answers to the only question the screen exists to answer.

## UX

### 0.4-004 — Panels are systematically taller than their content; the set reads as half-loaded

Present on every one of the eleven new screens, measured at 1440:

- `challenges-item` — the `Fund` / `Defer` / `Reject` cards are ~150 px tall holding one word
  each; the sender panel has ~120 px of empty space below the badge; the `Why?` panel ~100 px
  below its chips
- `people` — all six cards run ~100–150 px past their text
- `dashboard` — the Balanced Scorecard column ends ~330 px above the panel floor

Filed as **0.3-026** and unfixed; it has now propagated from three screens to fourteen. Read
cold, a bordered box with a heading and a large empty area looks like content that failed to
arrive. Whatever the fix is — content-height panels, or a filled state — it should land
before 0.5 turns these into components with fixed padding.

### 0.4-005 — Dashboard's value-chain coverage is a dense run-on, and "Weighted by your strategy" is stranded at the end

Rendered as two wrapped lines of small type:

```
Inbound 1/4 · Operations 2/4 · Outbound 4/5 · Marketing & sales 5/5 · Service 0/4
Firm infrastructure 3/4 · HR ok · Technology — · Procurement 0/3 · Weighted by your strategy
```

Nine activities and a legend in one undifferentiated string, in type visibly smaller than the
rest of the page. **Which** activities the strategy weights is unrecoverable — the phrase sits
at the end of the second line and attaches to nothing. This is finding **0.3-015** returning
in a new form: there it was a panel subtitle implying all activities were weighted, here it
is a trailing fragment implying the same. `strategy.html` names the weighted activities
explicitly, so the two screens disagree about what the reader can know.

### 0.4-006 — Challenges column headers are values, not labels

The table heads read `FROM · SUMMARY · RAISED ROUND 3 · RESPONDS BY LOCK · STATUS`, and every
row then repeats "Raised round 3" and "Responds by lock" in the cells beneath. The headers
should be `RAISED` and `RESPONDS BY`. As built, a column header asserts a value and its cells
restate it three times.

### 0.4-007 — Strategy shows five bare weights on a student screen

```
Where this strategy says value lies
Outbound logistics 0.35 · Operations 0.30 · Firm infrastructure 0.15 ·
Customer insight 0.10 · Marketing & sales 0.10
```

They sum to 1.000, so they are coherent and match `CONTRACTS.md`'s
`strategy.capability_weights{}`. But a normalised weight answers none of *what does it cost,
who does it affect, what happens if it fails* (`GOVERNANCE.md §2.1`). This is **0.3-017**,
which was closed as moot when the Situation screen was deleted; it has reappeared on
Strategy, now five times. **Owner: author** — `spec.md:270` fixes these values. `O3` settled
percentages for realised value and decimals only for the three factors that are multiplied
together; a strategy weight was never covered.
