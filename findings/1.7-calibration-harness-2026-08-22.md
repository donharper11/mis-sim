## Findings

### Report
- **1.7-A-001 (Report):** In the printed report, the Balanced-Scorecard and Term-Decomposition
  **column headers overflow their columns and run together** — e.g. `CustomerInternal
  ProcessLearning & Growth` and `TechnologyOrganisationManagement`. Root cause: `_fmt_row`
  right-justifies every cell to width 10 (`report.py:52`, `"".join(f"{c:>10}" for c in cells)`), but
  the labels "Internal Process" (16), "Learning & Growth" (17), and "Organisation" (12) exceed 10, so
  the headers do not align with their (5-char, correctly-aligned) data cells. **Non-blocking:** the
  numbers themselves are correct and correctly aligned, and the column order is fixed and documented
  (`_BSC_ORDER`, `_TERM_ORDER`); only the header labels are hard to map to columns by eye. Since the
  report is the instructor-facing deliverable for the human calibration review, widening the column
  (or abbreviating the headers) would improve legibility. Does NOT affect instrument faithfulness or
  the trustworthiness of any curve.
